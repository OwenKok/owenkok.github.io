GRAWLIZ · 2026
Original:
Grawliz explores the visual language of profanity, censorship and expression. Inspired by the symbols and exaggerated graphic marks traditionally used in comics to conceal offensive language, the collection transforms these familiar signs into functional objects. The symbols are enlarged, stretched, repeated and abstracted into furniture, lighting and sculptural pieces. Forms that once represented anger, frustration or vulgarity are reinterpreted through material, proportion and function, giving them a new identity. At its core, Grawliz questions how we define what is acceptable or offensive. What is considered vulgar in one culture may be humorous, familiar or harmless in another. Meaning is shaped by context, society and perception. By turning the language of censorship into objects of everyday use, Grawliz challenges the boundary between vulgarity and beauty, expression and restraint, asking where vulgarity ends and culture begins.

Edited:
Grawliz takes the visual shorthand of comic-book censorship—symbols used to replace profanity—and transforms it into furniture and objects. Enlarged and rearranged, these once-abrasive marks become playful sculptural forms. The collection questions how context changes meaning: what reads as offensive in one setting may be humorous or familiar in another. By giving the language of censorship an everyday function, Grawliz asks where vulgarity ends and culture begins.

INNER BEAST · 2022
Original:
Internal Struggle explores the opposing forces that exist within every individual instinct and restraint, aggression and calm, temptation and conscience. The collection transforms this psychological tension into physical form. Dark timber, sharp cuts, jagged openings and teeth-like details express a more primal and confrontational character, while the disciplined geometry, balance and functionality of each piece represent control and order. Rather than portraying good and evil as separate forces, the collection brings them together within the same object. Each piece becomes a reflection of the contradictions we carry within ourselves — a constant negotiation between impulse and reason. Internal Struggle is not about one side defeating the other, but about the tension created when both are forced to coexist.

Edited:
Inner Beast examines the opposing impulses within a person: instinct and restraint, aggression and calm, temptation and conscience. Angular cuts, dark surfaces and teeth-like details suggest a primal side, while measured proportions and functional forms provide a counterbalance. Each piece holds these tensions together rather than resolving them, reflecting the constant negotiation between impulse and control.

TORII STOOL · 2022
Original:
The introduction of Torii, a minimalist collection infused with the Japanese spirit and culture. The collection draws influence from the traditional Japanese gate which is typically found in Shinto shrines or the house of a family with a newborn baby. Japanese Torii gates are traditionally made from wood. In a similar approach, the Torii collection features exquisite pieces crafted from wood, a sturdy material to withstand daily usage in spaces. The pieces are characterised by the curve edged volumes, showcasing the grain and texture of the selected wood finish that expresses the balance between nature and human. With its pure and timeless lines, the minimalist appearance fits easily into any setting of your home. It can be placed beside the shoe cabinet or using it as a seat in the living area. It can also be an accent piece for the communal areas. The Torii is a simple yet subtly sophisticated collection, defined by its distinct proportions and traditional elements; the collection is a great addition to instil harmony and calmness to the space Salone Internazionale del Mobile Fiera Milano Rho 2023 by Moonler

Edited:
Torii Stool reinterprets the simple architectural gesture of the Japanese torii gate as an object for everyday use. Its curved timber volumes and purposeful proportions draw attention to the grain and character of the wood. Minimal yet distinctive, the stool seeks a balance between cultural reference, material and function. The work was presented by Moonler at Salone del Mobile.Milano in 2023.

HAMMER · 2018
Original:
Hammer explores the tension between strength and vulnerability. A heavy, solid timber mass is balanced by a slender extended arm and delicate platform, creating an intentional contrast between weight and lightness. Neither element exists independently. The strength of one supports the fragility of the other. Through this relationship, Hammer expresses balance not as equality but as opposing forces depending on one another to become whole.

Edited:
Hammer studies the relationship between strength and vulnerability. A weighty timber body is paired with an extended, delicate-looking arm and platform. The contrast creates a sense of tension, yet each part depends on the other. Here, balance is not achieved through sameness but through the coexistence of opposing forces.

BUSHIDO STOOL · 2007
Original:
The stool would be designed with clean, minimalist lines, and made of sturdy materials such as metal. The base of the stool would be designed to mimic the shape of a traditional Japanese torii gate, with two posts on either side of the seat. The koi design would be incorporated into the seat of the stool, with koi swimming towards each other in a circular pattern. The koi would be depicted in shades of blue, gold, and white, representing their symbolic meanings of perseverance and determination. The base of the stool would be painted in a deep red color, representing the courage and passion of the samurai warriors who followed bushido. The torii gate shape of the base would be enhanced with additional design elements such as cherry blossom motifs or kanji characters that represent the principles of bushido. Overall, a stool inspired by bushido with koi that change into a dragon print would be a powerful and meaningful addition to any space. Its combination of traditional Japanese symbolism and modern design elements ensures that it is both unique and timeless.

Edited:
Bushido Stool draws on Japanese symbolism and the values of perseverance and courage. The imagery of koi and their transformation into dragons provides a narrative layer, set against a restrained seating form. Traditional references are brought into dialogue with contemporary materials and proportion.
