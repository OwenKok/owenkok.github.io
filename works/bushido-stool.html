Bushido Stool

Perseverance and transformation through Japanese motifs.

Year2007
MaterialsAluminum
StageLimited edition
DESIGN STORY

Bushido Stool draws on Japanese symbolism and the values of perseverance and courage. The imagery of koi and their transformation into dragons provides a narrative layer, set against a restrained seating form. Traditional references are brought into dialogue with contemporary materials and proportion.
