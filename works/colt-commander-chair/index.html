Colt Commander Chair

Industrial force and everyday function in dialogue.

Year2016
MaterialsSteel and leather
StageLimited edition
DESIGN STORY

Colt Commander Chair continues the investigation of war and peace through an assertive, architectural seating form. Its steel structure and leather surfaces place industrial toughness alongside the intimacy of a chair. The work explores how conflicting ideas can inhabit a single object, shifting the language of force toward something made for human use.
