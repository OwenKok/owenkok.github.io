Oreo Table & Chair

A familiar cookie inspires a playful furniture pairing.

Year2005
MaterialsMDF and steel
StagePrototype
DESIGN STORY

Oreo Table & Chair reimagines the familiar sandwich cookie as a playful pair of furniture pieces. Layered edges and simple contrasting surfaces translate a nostalgic everyday reference into clean, approachable forms. The project reveals how an ordinary snack can become the starting point for design.
