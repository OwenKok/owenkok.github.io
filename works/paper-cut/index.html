Paper Cut

The language of paper cutting translated into a chair.

Year2012
MaterialsSteel and leather
StagePrototype
DESIGN STORY

Paper Cut takes inspiration from the openwork patterns of traditional Chinese paper cutting. Geometric lines and voids are reinterpreted through a contemporary armchair structure, bringing together delicacy and strength. The piece considers how a decorative language can become part of the furniture's form rather than an applied motif.
