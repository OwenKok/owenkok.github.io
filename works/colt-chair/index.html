Colt Chair

The materials of conflict redirected toward human use.

Year2016
MaterialsSteel and leather
StageLimited edition
DESIGN STORY

Colt Chair forms part of an exploration of war and peace through furniture. Industrial steel and leather bring together contrasting associations of force and comfort. By redirecting this material language into a chair, the design asks how objects associated with conflict might be understood through everyday use and human presence.
