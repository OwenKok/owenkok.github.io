Untitled 1

A contemporary study in thin lines, lightness and restraint.

Year2013
MaterialsSteel
StagePrototype
DESIGN STORY

Untitled 1 is a study in reduction. A slender steel profile, thin legs and a restrained seat-and-back composition give the chair a light, almost drawn silhouette. Rather than relying on ornament, the design is defined by proportion, balance and the quiet precision of its lines. It presents a contemporary idea of furniture that feels visually weightless.
