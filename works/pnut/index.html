Pnut

A playful furniture collection inspired by the peanut.

Year2009
MaterialsFibreglass
StagePrototype
DESIGN STORY

Pnut turns the rounded shape of a peanut into a family of playful furniture forms. Curved surfaces, compact proportions and vivid colour give the pieces an approachable personality. The collection finds humour in an everyday reference while keeping each object recognisable and functional.
