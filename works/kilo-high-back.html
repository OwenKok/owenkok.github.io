Kilo High Back

A dramatic silhouette in steel and leather.

Year2016
MaterialsSteel and leather
StageLimited edition
DESIGN STORY

Kilo High Back gives a familiar chair a strong visual presence through the extended line of its backrest. Steel and leather bring together industrial strength and the tactility of seating. Its poised silhouette belongs to a wider exploration of tension and reconciliation within the idea of war and peace.
