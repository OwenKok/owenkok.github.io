About

Owen Kok

Owen Kok is a Singapore-based designer whose practice spans interior architecture, furniture, product design and creative direction. Trained at Nanyang Academy of Fine Arts, his work is driven by a belief that design should communicate an idea beyond function and aesthetics.

Expression through Impression.
PRACTICE & APPROACH

His approach often begins with observations of human behaviour, culture and everyday contradictions, translating them into objects that are expressive, playful and sometimes provocative. Through proportion, material, humour and unexpected forms, Owen uses furniture as a medium for storytelling, allowing familiar objects to carry new meanings and invite different interpretations.

His philosophy, “Expression through Impression,” reflects an approach in which an object first captures attention through its presence, before gradually revealing the thought, emotion or narrative behind it.

Over the years, Owen's furniture and collectible works have been presented internationally, including exhibitions in Milan, Paris and New York, alongside collaborations with furniture manufacturers and design brands. His body of work ranges from experimental collectible pieces to commercially developed furniture, while maintaining a distinctive visual identity.

Beyond product design, Owen is also the co-founder and Creative Director of Mark Owen Interior Associates, where he brings the same conceptual thinking into interior architecture, creating spaces defined by strong ideas, material sensitivity and a clear design point of view.

Across both objects and spaces, his work consistently explores one central idea: design should not simply be seen or used; it should leave an impression.
